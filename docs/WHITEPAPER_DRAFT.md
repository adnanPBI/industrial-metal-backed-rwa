# ReserveChain.io Institutional Whitepaper - Stage 1 Draft Structure

**Status:** In preparation. Proposed/pending sections remain subject to owner, legal, asset, custody, insurance, valuation and technical approval.

## 1. Executive overview
ReserveChain is developing infrastructure intended to connect documented industrial-metal assets with digital asset identity, evidence, controlled custody, reserve reconciliation and future tokenization/redemption workflows.

## 2. Initial industrial-metal programs
- Ultrafine Copper Powder - supplied IGAS material evidence reports 99.9999% purity.
- Nickel Wire 0.025 mm - supplied IGAS material evidence reports 99.9807% purity.

These source documents do not independently establish present ownership, custody, insurance or reserve coverage.

## 3. Physical-to-digital lifecycle
Physical Asset -> Verify -> Value -> Custody -> Digital Asset Passport -> Tokenize -> Reconcile -> Participate -> Redeem.

## 4. Asset Registry and Digital Asset Passports
Data model for programs, lots/batches, containers/coils, laboratory records, ownership, valuation, custody, insurance, reserve status, token series and redemption history.

## 5. Proposed legal and corporate structure
**Pending owner/legal-adviser information.** The source package contains an Estonia/Swiss wording conflict that must be resolved before final publication.

## 6. Verification, custody and insurance
Proposed workflows are implemented as separate evidence/status domains. Final providers and approved publication claims are pending.

## 7. Token architecture
ERC-20 on Ethereum is the stated target architecture. Name, symbol, cap/supply, ratios, commercial rights, pricing and mainnet addresses remain configurable and unpublished until approved.

## 8. Proof of Reserves
Proposed reconciliation model for eligible physical units, issued/circulating/burned supply, supporting evidence, attestations, exceptions and historical reporting. No live reserve ratio is claimed in the current stage.

## 9. Compliance and jurisdiction controls
Configurable KYC/KYB, AML, sanctions, jurisdiction, eligibility and transfer controls. Final legal rules and providers are pending owner approval.

## 10. Redemption
Proposed request -> eligibility -> compliance recheck -> settlement/burn -> custody release -> logistics -> completion workflow. Final rights, thresholds, fees, taxes and delivery terms are pending.

## 11. Technology and security
WordPress public CMS plus canonical structured registry/API; future isolated compliance/blockchain/reconciliation services; RBAC, MFA, audit trails, secure document storage, monitoring, backups and independent testing.

## 12. Governance, risks and roadmap
Governance controls, maker-checker approvals, issuer-controlled infrastructure, risk categories and staged 90-120 day implementation.

## 13. Disclosures
No token sale, investment allocation, live reserve, guaranteed liquidity, confirmed redemption right or return is represented by this Stage 1 draft.
