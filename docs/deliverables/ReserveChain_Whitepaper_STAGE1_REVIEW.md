## Mandatory pre-launch notice
ReserveChain is in development. No tokens are offered or sold through this website. Registration does not reserve tokens or an asset, promise allocation, fix a price, or confer future participation. Jurisdictional availability is subject to definitive legal documents, KYC/KYB and sanctions controls; seek counsel for exact exclusion text.

## 1. Proposed project
The intended system links industrial-metal inventory to documentary evidence, Digital Asset Passports, custody, proposed Proof-of-Reserves reconciliation and potentially future ERC-20 tokenization. These are implementation plans and are not fully operating today.

## 2. Corporate structure
The Swiss issuer and holding arrangements are described as **in formation** in the supplied whitepaper, but earlier instructions also mention Estonia. The owner and counsel must resolve this before publishing affirmative jurisdiction or offer language.

## 3. Proposed metal programs
- Copper Powder — source-stated 99.9999% purity, IGAS assay reference 0004512 (2022-07-04), lot #03-K-07. The 2000 kg* figure is customer-reported historical inventory information, **not independently verified eligible reserves**.
- Nickel Wire — source-stated 99.9807% purity and 0.025 mm diameter, IGAS assay reference 0004368 (2021-10-19), lot 120/NP1. The 5000 g* figure across 30 bobbins is customer-reported and not proof of present custody.

## 4. Proposed passports and reserves
Future production records would link individually verified containers/coils with chain of title, assay provenance, custody, valuation and signed hash proofs. A daily independently corroborated 17-ledger reconciliation system has **not been deployed or demonstrated**.

## 5. Smart contracts and USDT
The repository has developer smart-contract prototypes and a test harness. No independent audited mainnet issuance, owner-signatory approval, USDT acquisition launch, vesting/treasury operations or live token sale has been proven. These remain disabled.

## 6. Proposed physical redemption
Future redemption will require authenticated token-holder consent, current title/weight/serial verification, KYC/sanctions, applicable fees, dual approval, contract-level safeguards and signed custodian release. Current flows are illustrative, not redeemable.

## 7. Risk and final approvals
Key risks include valuation, authenticity, asset custody, counterparty, insurance exclusions, legal restrictions, smart-contract flaws, wallet loss and liquidity. No guarantee of value or redemption is made. Final publication requires owner/legal approval, qualified independent assurance and completed production acceptance.
